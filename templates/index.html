How Shardkeep works

  1. Chunking — every upload is split into 256 KB pieces.
  2. Load-balanced placement — each chunk's primary and replica copy go to whichever nodes currently hold the least data.
  3. Replication — every chunk exists on two nodes at once, across a 4-node cluster.
  4. Integrity checks — SHA-256 hashes catch corrupted chunks on download and fall back to the replica.
  5. Self-healing — mark a node offline and Shardkeep automatically re-replicates its chunks onto a healthy node.
  6. Hybrid cloud backup — optionally keep a full off-cluster copy as a last-resort restore path.
  7. Secure sharing — generate public links with optional password protection, expiry, and download limits.
